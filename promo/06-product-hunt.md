# Product Hunt

Запускать после первых отзывов с других площадок. Вторник–четверг, 00:01 по Тихоокеанскому времени (10:01 МСК).
Нужно: 3–5 скриншотов/GIF (сетка с «золотым окном», «скопировать для чата», шаринг ссылкой, тёмная тема, язык),
иконка 240×240 (жёлтый круг). Не просить апвоуты массовой рассылкой — PH это наказывает; можно сообщить
своим подписчикам/знакомым, что вы запустились, и попросить отзыв.

**Name:** Timezone Sync

**Tagline (≤ 60):** Find a meeting time that works in every time zone

Варианты: `Everyone's real hours → one shared meeting window` · `Meeting planner for teams across time zones`

**Topics:** Productivity, Remote Work, Meetings, Free, Open Source

**Description (≤ 260):**
Add people, set each person's real working hours, and see the best shared window instantly — even across midnight
and half-hour zones. Copy a ready line for chat, share the setup as a link. Free, no sign-up, 10 languages, open source.

**Первый комментарий мейкера:**

Hi Product Hunt 👋

I made Timezone Sync because scheduling our weekly call across [N] time zones kept turning into a thread of
time-zone math — and someone always ended up with a 7am or 11pm slot.

What makes it different from clock-comparison tools:
• **Everyone sets their own hours** — gaps and half-hours included, not a fixed 9–5
• **The answer comes first** — best shared window at the top, with "starts in…"
• **Copy for chat** — one line with every attendee's local time
• **No accounts** — share the setup as a link; optional phone↔laptop sync is end-to-end encrypted
• 10 languages, dark mode, MIT-licensed

It's free. I'd love to hear what's missing for your team — calendar integration? recurring rotations? Tell me 🙏
