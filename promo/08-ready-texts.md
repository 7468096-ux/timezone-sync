**Categories / tags:** Time zone converter, Meeting planner, World clock, Remote work, No registration required, Privacy focused, Dark mode
**Alternative to:** World Time Buddy, Every Time Zone, timeanddate.com (Meeting Planner), Timezone.io

**Short description (≤ 150):**
Find a meeting time across time zones. Each person sets their own working hours and you see the best shared window right away.

**Long description:**
I made it because I work with clients in other countries and we always lost time on "what time is it for you?".

How it works: add people, set the hours when each person is really available (not only 9 to 5, gaps and half hours are ok). On top you see the best shared window and how soon it starts. "Copy for chat" gives one line with the time for every person, ready for Slack or Telegram.

- No sign-up, free
- Share the whole setup with one link (the data is in the link itself, not on a server)
- Works across midnight and with half-hour zones like India
- Sync between phone and laptop, end-to-end encrypted
- 10 languages, light and dark theme
- Open source: https://github.com/7468096-ux/timezone-sync
